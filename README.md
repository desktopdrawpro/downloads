# DesktopDraw PRO

**Draw directly on your screen in Windows.**

DesktopDraw PRO is a professional screen annotation app for Windows PC. Draw, highlight, point and explain anything on top of any program, video or website during presentations, classes, online meetings, live streams and screen recordings.

Made for teachers, streamers, YouTube creators, presenters, remote support, designers and developers.

## ✨ Features

### Drawing & shapes
- Freehand pen with smooth, natural strokes, plus highlighter and progressive pen
- Brush textures: chalk, pencil, charcoal, crayon, felt marker and watercolor
- 360° arrows, straight lines, rectangles, ellipses and circles, with outline and fill
- Shape recognition: draw roughly and it turns into a clean shape, with an optional countdown
- Editable vector shapes: select, reshape and recolor them at any time
- Rich vector text: fonts, sizes, bold, italic and underline, always sharp
- Speech bubbles, connector lines, and numbered or lettered stamps (1, 2, 3 / A, B, C)
- Emoji stickers and images
- Symbol libraries, all sharp vector symbols you can rotate: astrology, Kabbalah, tarot, alchemy, runes, numerology, sacred geometry, chakras, math, physics & chemistry, music, geography, arrows & callouts, flowcharts, business and gaming
- Load your own `.ddlib` symbol libraries

### Colors & style
- Color selector, separate outline and fill colors, line thickness and transparency
- Drawing presets and favorites
- Brush pointer styles, with adjustable size, outline and shadow

### Edit & transform
- Eraser, clear the active layer, and undo / redo
- Selection tools: rectangle, lasso and circle
- Move, rotate and scale, with alignment helpers and 15° / 5% steps
- Group transform, Photoshop-style shortcuts, and double-click to re-edit
- Redact (blur or hide sensitive areas)

### Layers
- Transparent screen overlay that switches between mouse mode and drawing mode
- Layer management: show / hide, lock, opacity, and Photoshop-style layer groups
- Vectors preserved for crisp editing

### Whiteboards, projects & export
- White and black whiteboards with size, position, technical grid and multiple pages
- Paper flip chart with as many sheets as you need, plus a floating board menu
- Screenshots of the full screen, an area or a window
- Snippet to annotate: capture an area, draw on it, then save or copy it
- Paste images inside or outside boards, or as a new layer
- Save / open projects, PNG export, and PDF export of boards and flip charts

### Presentation tools
- Spotlight, magnifier, laser dot and laser trail
- Click highlights and on-screen keys (Show keys)
- Presentation toolbar, presentation timer and sports stopwatch
- Ruler and 180° / 360° protractor that keep strokes perfectly straight or on the arc
- Fading ink that disappears by itself after the seconds you choose
- Visual monitor picker for multi-monitor setups

### Compact mode & radial menu
- Compact toolbar, horizontal or vertical, dockable to any screen edge
- Radial menu: right-click while drawing to get tools and colors at the pointer

### Live streaming: Send To OBS
- The compact toolbar as a panel docked inside OBS Studio, with one-click setup
- The panel stays clickable above the drawing
- Optionally open DesktopDraw from OBS even when it is closed
- Runs locally on your PC; nothing goes online

### Customization & system
- Custom hotkeys, multi-monitor support and flexible layouts
- Themes and 6 languages: English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Italian
- Windows & audio controls: volume, mute, microphone, play / pause, calculator, notepad and File Explorer
- Quick-launch shortcuts for programs, folders, documents and websites
- Guided welcome tour and built-in update notices
